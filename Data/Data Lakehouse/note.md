### The Unified Namespace (UNS)
- The Unified Namespace (UNS) offers an alternative approach to traditional industrial data architecture.
- Traditional methods typically follow the **ISA-95** pyramidal network-and-system architectural model, which relies on propagating data upwards for centralized storage and subsequent analysis.
- Spoke–hub distribution paradigm : مدل یا شبکه یا پارادایم توزیع قطب و اقماری
